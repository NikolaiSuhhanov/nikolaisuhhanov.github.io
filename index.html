About Me

Medicine meets software development

I am a Unity Developer with a professional background in general surgery and more than eight years of clinical experience.

After working in abdominal surgery, I decided to transition into Medical Technology to contribute to medical education, simulation and the development of interactive healthcare software.

I am currently studying Web Technologies while developing Unity applications for Windows and Android. My long-term interests include Medical XR, surgical planning, medical visualization, DICOM and image-guided navigation.
